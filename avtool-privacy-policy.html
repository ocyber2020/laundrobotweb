01Scope

This policy covers AVTool, the iOS application and companion web dashboard published by Intellicue ("we", "us"). It explains how we handle information about people who install, sign in to, or use AVTool.

It does not cover third-party services you reach through AVTool. When you follow a link out of the app, the receiving service's own policy applies.

How we collect it

  • You give it to us — when you create an account, contact support, or enter content into the app.
  • Automatically from the app — through our own instrumentation as you use features. We do not embed third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • From Apple — purchase and subscription status through StoreKit, and crash reports if you have opted in to sharing them with developers.

Tracking

AVTool collects usage analytics, but it does not track you. Tracking has a specific meaning here: linking your data with data collected about you by other companies' apps or websites for advertising or advertising measurement, or sharing your data with data brokers. We do none of that.

Our analytics are first-party. Events are recorded by our own instrumentation and sent to servers we control. They are not passed to an advertising network, an attribution service, or any other third party. AVTool does not access the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and for that reason does not present the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect precise location.
  • We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under California law.

03How we use it

  • Run the service. Authenticate you, sync settings, deliver the features you request.
  • Keep it working. Investigate crashes, errors, and performance regressions.
  • Keep it safe. Detect abuse, fraud, and unauthorised access attempts.
  • Support. Respond when you contact us, using the context you give us.
  • Legal obligations. Meet tax, accounting, and lawful-request requirements.

We do not use your content to train machine learning models. Remove this sentence if it is not accurate.

04Legal basis (UK & EU users)

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide AVTool once you have an account.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the service, prevent abuse, and improve reliability, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — for optional analytics and any marketing email. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings › Privacy.
  • Legal obligation — where retention or disclosure is required by law.

06How long we keep it

  • Account data: for as long as your account is active, then 30 days after deletion.
  • Diagnostic and crash data: 90 days.
  • Aggregate usage statistics: retained indefinitely in a form that cannot identify you.
  • Billing records: 7 years, as required for tax purposes.

07Security

Data in transit is protected with TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at rest on our servers is encrypted with AES-256. On device, credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain and application files use iOS Data Protection.

Encryption and export compliance

AVTool uses only the standard encryption provided by the operating system and by HTTPS. It contains no proprietary or non-standard cryptography. This declaration corresponds to the export-compliance answer filed with Apple; if the app's cryptography changes, both must be updated.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify affected users and the relevant supervisory authority within the timeframes the law requires.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent you previously gave.

Deleting your account from inside the app

If you created an account in AVTool, you can delete it and the data associated with it without contacting us. Open Settings › Account › Delete Account in the app and confirm. Deletion removes your account from our servers, not only from your device. Anything we must keep for legal reasons is listed in section 06.

Withdrawing consent

Optional analytics can be turned off at any time in Settings › Privacy. Turning it off stops future collection; to remove what was already collected, use the deletion request below.

Send requests to privacy@ocybersecurity.com. We respond within 30 days and will not charge you or degrade your service for exercising a right.

UK and EU users may also complain to their data protection authority. California residents may designate an authorised agent to make a request on their behalf.

09Children

AVTool is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10International transfers

We operate from the United States. If you use AVTool from outside that region, your data is transferred there. For transfers from the UK or EEA we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum, together with additional technical safeguards.

11Changes to this policy

When we make a material change we will update the effective date above and notify you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. Continued use after that date means you accept the updated policy.
