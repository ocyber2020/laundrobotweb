04Troubleshooting

The app crashes on launch

  1. Force quit AVTool: swipe up from the bottom of the screen and swipe the app away.
  2. Restart the device.
  3. Check for an AVTool update in the App Store — the fix may already be shipped.
  4. If it persists, delete and reinstall. Note: local data not yet synced will be lost.

I can't sign in

  1. Confirm the email address, including its domain.
  2. Use the reset link on the sign-in screen; the email arrives within a few minutes.
  3. Check spam and any corporate mail filtering.
  4. On a managed or corporate network, confirm outbound HTTPS to our API domain isn't blocked.

Something looks wrong or out of date

  1. Pull to refresh on the affected screen.
  2. Confirm the device has a working connection.
  3. Sign out and back in to force a fresh sync.
